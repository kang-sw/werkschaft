Werkschaft — Windows title strip v2 mockup · drawer tone

Same layout as v1, re-toned as one full-width drawer handle. Height 34px (v1: 32): the drawer's tabs, .tph and the header are all 34px (--toph), so the strip and the tabs hanging under it share one rhythm; the native Win11 caption is 32px, the 2px is the price of that alignment. Hover and press every label and caption button in any frame.

Drawer rules per part — strip fill/radii/shadow: .dr-handle/.gh (--wb-card, 0 0 8px 8px, --wb-shadow-hi); text: 12px / 700 / --wb-muted-fg → --wb-fg on :hover; label hover surface: .ghead's --wb-muted at .wtrow's 6px radius; menu-open state: .gh.open's 2px --wb-primary underline; focus ring: 1.5px --wb-primary, -2px offset; caption hover: --wb-muted (close keeps #c42b1c); unfocused: drops to --wb-shadow.

Chosen behaviour: menu labels never shrink or hide; the title text is the only element that gives way (text-overflow: ellipsis, then to nothing, leaving the icon), and the drag area keeps a floor of 48px. At 720px everything fits and the drag area is still ~133px. Below ~635px the title ellipsizes (600px frame: "We…"); below 600px it is dropped and only the icon remains (560px frame). 560px is the recommended minimum window width: icon + six labels + 48px drag + three caption buttons.
