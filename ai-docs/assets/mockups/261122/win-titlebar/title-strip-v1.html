Height 32px (Win11 caption-bar height at 100% scaling; the dashboard's own header handles are 34px and hang below it). Fill is the dashboard's --wb-bg, so the strip and the page are one surface separated by a half-strength hairline. Menu labels open the existing native menus as popups, so only their hover/pressed state is drawn here. Everything is live: hover and press the labels and caption buttons in any frame.

Chosen behaviour: menu labels never shrink or hide; the title text is the only element that gives way (text-overflow: ellipsis, then to nothing, leaving the icon), and the drag area keeps a floor of 48px. At 720px everything fits and the drag area is still ~139px. Below ~630px the title ellipsizes (600px frame: "Werks…"); below 600px it is dropped and only the icon remains (560px frame). 560px is the recommended minimum window width: icon + six labels + 48px drag + three caption buttons.
